# P4-0: the Cash page, before building

Date: 26 Nov 2026. Branch: `phase-4`, from `main` (which has phase-3, PR #4).

## Is a migration needed? No.

**What the chart needs:**
- the month-end cash for up to the last 12 month-ends with balances;
- the months that have balances at all (a month with balances but no cash row is 0; a month never
  imported is not drawn).

**Everything it needs is already readable** under the user's own row security, with no new function
and no grant change. The viewer policy and the SELECT grants on `balances_monthly`, `line_map` and
`sync_runs` were checked on 26 Nov 2026.

| Need | Read |
|---|---|
| Which accounts are Cash and bank | `fpa.balance_accounts(entity)` (already used by Map accounts and Connect): rows with `plan_line = 'cash_bank'`. A handful of accounts |
| Which months have balances | The balances import log (`sync_runs`, kind `balances`), as Connect already reads it. Every month in an import is replaced whole and has at least one row, so the imported months are exactly the months with balances |
| The closing balances | `balances_monthly`, filtered by connection, by those few account codes, and to the last 12 imported months. That is at most about 12 × (number of cash accounts) rows (Test 2: 24), read a page at a time. It uses the existing index on (connection, period) |

The sums are done in `src/engine`, in exact cents.

**The figures stay identical to the KPI strip:**
- Cash now (cash, burn, runway) comes from `fpa.cash_position` → `cashKpis`, the strip's own path,
  and is not computed a second time.
- A test checks that the history's latest month equals `cash_position`'s cash.

**At 20,000 rows (2,000 accounts × 10 months) this does not get heavy.** Only the cash-mapped
accounts are read, never the whole balance sheet. So `fpa.cash_history` is not needed. If an entity
ever had hundreds of bank accounts, a 0005 invoker function would be the cleaner answer; I am not
building it now.

## Conflicts and decisions (what I am doing, and why)

1. **The projection starts after the latest month with balances, not today.**
   - Test 2's balances end in Dec 2025 and today is Nov 2026. So "the next 12 months" is Jan–Dec
     2026, mostly in the past.
   - The page says so: "Projection from the latest balances (Dec 2025)".
   - Projecting from today would mean inventing 11 months of cash.
2. **The scenarios' budget year can differ from the projection year.**
   - Test 2's scenarios are on "Budget 2025 (stretch)", a 2025 budget; the projection runs over 2026.
   - I apply the scenario's annual net-profit difference against Base evenly over the 12 projected
     months, as the prompt says, and label the budget's year: "Profit difference from Budget 2025
     (stretch), Jan–Dec 2025, spread evenly".
   - **For you to confirm later.** Using a 2025 budget's profit as a proxy for 2026 is a modelling
     choice, and the label makes it visible.
3. **Cents.**
   - Current trend: cash at month k = cash now − k × the net cash burn shown, rounded to cents as the
     KPI strip shows it. For Test 2 the burn is exactly €12,150.00.
   - Scenario adjustment: the cumulative difference is rounded once per month, as k × difference ÷
     12, half away from zero. It is never rounded per step, so nothing drifts. All bigint cents.
4. **"Runs out"** is the first projected month below zero, not at zero. Test 2's current trend:
   95,400 − 12,150 × 8 < 0, so **Aug 2026** (month 8), as you said.
5. **Not burning cash:** the projection rises, and the page says "Cash does not run out within 12
   months". **Cash at or below zero while burning:** runway 0.0, and it runs out in month 1.
6. **Fewer than four consecutive month-ends:** burn and runway show "—" (the shared engine), and no
   projection is drawn, with the reason. Scenarios and the what-if then have nothing to adjust, so
   they say so.
7. **The what-if** only changes the Current trend line and its table, in the browser. Scenarios stay
   based on the actual burn.
8. **The viewer change.** Your prompt says another change adds a viewer to Walkthrough Test 2. That
   change has already run: Sean's viewer row, `docs/reports/P4-SEAN.md` on branch `p4-sean`. I will
   not touch `fpa.entity_members`.
