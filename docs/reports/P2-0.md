## 1. Conflicts and gaps, with what I propose

Items marked **(DB)** affect 0004. I build them as proposed, because nothing is applied before your
review at P2-A and each can still change. Items marked **(UI)** come in 2A/2B and change nothing in
the database.

1. **(DB) `fpa.variance` would show "Cash and bank" on Compare.**
   - It returns every plan line except `excluded` (`WHERE d.kind <> 'excluded'`).
   - A new `balance` line would appear on Compare as its own line, holding the P&L movements of any
     account mapped to it. Your rule is that it never appears in Compare.
   - **Proposed:** 0004 replaces `fpa.variance` with the same text except
     `WHERE d.kind NOT IN ('excluded', 'balance')`.
   - Calculated lines already add only `income` and `cost`, and budgets only `budgetable` lines, so
     neither changes.
   - **The proof that nothing else changes:** a test-only copy of the 0003 `variance` compared row for
     row with the new one. They are identical when no account is mapped to "Cash and bank"; when one
     is, the only difference is the missing Cash and bank line.
2. **(DB) Balance-only accounts can't be mapped today.**
   - `fpa.account_map` lists accounts that have **P&L actuals** or a mapping. An account that appears
     only in a balances file (for example "Bank deposit") would never reach the mapping screen.
   - **Proposed:** a new function, `fpa.balance_accounts(entity_id)`: invoker, paged, with the same
     columns and order rules as `account_map`, but reading `balances_monthly`.
   - **Why not change `account_map`:** that would change a Phase 1 function's output, and the Phase 1
     screens and tests depend on it.
   - The mapping screen gets a **Balance sheet accounts** section built on it.
3. **(DB) One mapping per account code.** `line_map` is per connection and account code, shared by P&L
   and balances.
   - The sample P&L file already contains "1200 Bank current account" (mapped to excluded in the
     walk-through).
   - If someone maps 1200 to "Cash and bank", its P&L movements leave the excluded total and Compare.
     That is correct, because cash is not P&L.
   - **Proposed (UI):**
     - "Cash and bank" is offered in the balance section only;
     - a P&L account already mapped to it shows that, with a short note;
     - nothing is ever re-mapped automatically, as your decision says.
4. **(UI, wording) "Actuals through" against "balances through".**
   - Your decision says the Cash balance is taken "at the latest month of actuals", and the 2B text
     says it shows "Actuals through [month]".
   - Balances are a separate file, and can lag (or lead) the P&L actuals.
   - **Proposed:**
     - Cash balance = cash at the **latest month with balances**;
     - the tile says "**Balances through [month]**", and also "Actuals through [month]" if the two
       differ;
     - `cash_position` returns that latest balance month, so either rule is possible.
   - **Please confirm, or keep "latest month of actuals"** (then the tile shows "—" until balances
     reach that month).
5. **(UI) "Fewer than four month-end balances" against the formula's months.**
   - The burn needs cash at month m and month m−3, and m moves back one month when the latest is the
     current calendar month.
   - So four month-ends can exist and still not include m−3. Example: balances Aug–Nov, with Nov being
     this month, gives m = Oct, which needs Jul.
   - **Proposed:** Runway and burn show "—" unless month-end balances exist for **each of m−3, m−2,
     m−1 and m** (four in a row). That matches the wording and never guesses a missing month.
   - `cash_position` returns the four month-ends m−3..m, with nulls where missing, and the total count.
6. **(UI) Cash at or below zero.**
   - **Proposed:** while burning cash, if the cash balance is zero or negative (an overdraft), Runway
     shows "**0.0**". Truncating a negative figure would give a negative runway, which means nothing.
   - **Please confirm.**
7. **(DB) A month in the balances file with no row for a cash account.**
   - Whole-month replace makes each month's file the full picture for that connection, so an account
     with no row in an imported month has a **zero** balance that month.
   - A month that was never imported has **no** balance, which gives "—" in the burn, not zero.
   - **Proposed** as described. The import preview says so.
8. **(DB) Balances and the connection's dates.**
   - `import_actuals` sets `connections.actuals_through` and `last_synced_at`, which Compare shows.
   - **Proposed:** `import_balances` leaves the connection row alone, so Compare's "Actuals through …,
     last synced …" keeps meaning the P&L.
   - The balance dates come from `balances_monthly` and from `sync_runs` rows of kind `balances`.
   - `sync_runs` gains `kind` (`actuals` or `balances`), defaulting to `actuals` so `import_actuals` is
     unchanged.
9. **(UI) Unmapped balance accounts.**
   - Cash counts only accounts mapped to "Cash and bank", so an unmapped bank account would silently
     lower the cash.
   - **Proposed:** while any balance account is unmapped, the Cash balance tile says
     "**Incomplete: N balance accounts unmapped**", in the same way Net profit does.
   - Balance accounts can be mapped to "Cash and bank" or "Not in the P&L (excluded)". Excluded
     accounts stay excluded, and they are not cash.
10. **(DB) Extending the plan-line list in place.**
    - **The two kind columns:** `mappable` and `budgetable` are generated columns. Both are targets of
      composite foreign keys from `line_map` and `plan_lines`.
    - **On PostgreSQL 17,** `ALTER TABLE … ALTER COLUMN mappable SET EXPRESSION AS (kind IN ('income',
      'cost', 'excluded', 'balance'))` changes it in place, without dropping those keys.
    - `budgetable` is unchanged.
    - **The kind check** gains `balance`.
    - **The new row:** `('cash_bank', 'Cash and bank', 'balance', 140, 1)`. A debit (asset) balance
      shows positive.
    - **Rollback:** it refuses while any mapping points at `cash_bank`, or any balances exist, unless
      confirmed.
11. **(DB) The all-or-nothing budget save: `fpa.save_budget_lines(version_id, lines jsonb)`.**
    - Invoker; editor or owner; refused if the version is locked (PT423).
    - Every line must be an `{id, amount}` of that version's **base scenario**, with no duplicates, at
      most 1,200 lines, and amounts in the same text format as `import_actuals`.
    - It sets each amount and `origin = 'manual'`, and returns the count. Any problem refuses all of it.
12. **(DB) Re-running the Phase 1 suite on top of 0004.**
    - `test_0003.sql` runs 0003 inside its own transaction, so it can't run again now that 0003 is
      applied.
    - **Proposed:** a new bundle, `test_0003_on_0004.sql`: `BEGIN; 0004; the unchanged Phase 1 test
      file`. That runs every Phase 1 check against the live 0001 and 0003 plus 0004.
    - **Expected to differ, listed in advance; any other difference is a failure:**
      - "plan_line_defs holds exactly the agreed list": now 14 lines;
      - the six "plan_line_defs read as …" counts: 14, not 13;
      - "16 tables with row security": now 17;
      - (added while testing) the two "variance, new and old, identical" checks that compare with the
        test's own copy of the pre-0003 variance (`fpa_test.old_variance`). That copy keeps every line
        that is not `excluded`, so once Cash and bank exists it adds a zero Cash and bank line that the
        new variance correctly leaves out. The 0004 test shows the same thing directly against the
        applied 0003 text (section 9).
    - The Phase 1 test's fixtures are its own made-up users and entities, so the live test data is
      invisible to them. If a live row does affect a count, I report it rather than editing the test.
