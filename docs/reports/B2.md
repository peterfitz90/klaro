## What 0001 creates

- **14 tables** as specified, each with `id uuid`, `created_at`, `updated_at` (trigger), row security
  on, REVOKE ALL first and then only these grants:
  - `authenticated` gets SELECT on 13 tables (not `schema_migrations`).
  - Column-level INSERT/UPDATE on the writable columns only. Parent keys (`entity_id`, `plan_id`, …)
    are never updatable.
  - DELETE on the 8 plan tables.
- **40 policies**, all `TO authenticated`:
  - Viewers read.
  - Editors and owners write plan data.
  - Owners rename the entity and add or disable connections.
- **anon and service_role: nothing at all**, not even USAGE on the schema.
- **Helpers:**
  - `is_entity_member(entity, min_role)` plus five `member_for_*` boolean helpers and
    `member_in_jurisdiction` (all SECURITY DEFINER, STABLE, `search_path = fpa, pg_temp`,
    authenticated only).
  - Five `*_entity` lookups, executable by no API role.
- **Functions (authenticated):**
  - `create_entity`: entity, owner membership and default plan in one go; cap of 10; per-user lock.
  - `add_member` and `set_member`: owner only; at least one active owner always kept.
- **Integrity across entities:**
  - Composite foreign keys keep plan lines, packs and "based on" versions inside their own
    version or plan, and actuals inside their connection's entity.
  - A trigger keeps a driver value's driver and scenario in the same plan.
  - There are no foreign keys out of `fpa`.
- **Locked versions:** once `locked_at` is set, the version's lines, scenarios, values and packs
  can't change, and the version can't be renamed or removed until it is unlocked (PT423).

## Deviations from the prompt, and why

1. **`schema_migrations` also has `id`, `created_at` and `updated_at`.** The prompt asked for these
   on every table; `filename` is UNIQUE.
2. **`plans.fiscal_year_start` is a month (1–12), not a date.** The create form asks for a month.
3. **Values I had to choose for enumerations the prompt didn't list:**
   - `vat_scheme`: standard, cash_basis, flat_rate, not_registered
   - `connections.status`: pending, active, error, disabled
   - `drivers.unit`: currency, percent, count, months, days, ratio
   - `drivers.kind`: input, calculated
   - `vat_rules.filing_frequency`: monthly, bi_monthly, quarterly, four_monthly, six_monthly, annual

   **These are your call**; they're easy to change before apply.
4. **`vat_cycle_anchor` is nullable** (unknown until set), rather than defaulting to January.
5. **VAT rules are readable only when verified**, and only by active members of an entity in that
   jurisdiction. That fails closed: until you verify the rules, the app sees none. It also meets
   "a non-member sees zero rows everywhere".
6. **`entities` can't be deleted by anyone through the API in Phase 0**, and jurisdiction and
   currency can't be changed after creation (changing them would corrupt stored amounts).
7. **Owners, not editors, add connections.** Status can only be set back to pending or disabled.
   `active`, `last_synced_at` and `actuals_through` are left for the Phase 1 sync function.
8. **Driver values are `numeric(18,6)`, not `(14,2)`**, because drivers include rates and counts.
   Money stays `numeric(14,2)`. There are no floats anywhere; a test checks this.
9. **Locked versions** (above) aren't in the prompt. I added the lock because `locked_at` otherwise
   means nothing. Editors can unlock; say if that should be owner-only.
10. **"Two parallel calls cannot slip past the cap"** is proven by mechanism, not by a real race:
    - The test confirms `create_entity` holds a per-user transaction advisory lock until commit.
    - The connector runs one transaction at a time, so it can't run two calls in parallel.
    - A true concurrent test (two API calls at once) belongs in B5, the way Trinity moved its
      concurrency test to B3.
11. **Two test runs were sent in a slightly reduced form** (see B2-tests.txt, runs 8 and 9). The
    results are unaffected.

## What I would change, or what you might want

- The `driver_values` same-plan trigger runs before row security. A non-member gets PT400 ("must
  belong to the same plan") instead of 42501. It's still refused and leaks nothing, but the error
  code differs.
- Ledgrly-style KPI maps and per-user report views can wait until Phase 1.
- The 0002 VAT draft holds 9 unverified rows as reference points only (IE 23 / 13.5 / 9 / 0 with
  bi-monthly filing due the 19th or 23rd; UK 20 / 5 / 0 with quarterly filing due one month and
  7 days after the period). **`effective_from` is a placeholder (2025-01-01).** Every row needs your
  check against Revenue and GOV.UK, and it becomes a new numbered migration once verified.
